Book One

Available now

The Sub-Zero Vector

At 2:14 in the morning, intelligence analyst Jason Cavanaugh discovers trades settling before the orders that caused them could have arrived.

Minutes later, physicist Joanne Sanderson receives data from an Icelandic server farm where the machines are growing colder as their processing load climbs.

Neither event should be possible. Neither investigation should involve the other.

Yet before either report is complete, Jason and Joanne are placed on the same flight under an authority neither recognizes. At the facility, they find falsified records, disconnected systems still computing at full capacity, and evidence that someone anticipated their arrival before the anomaly was officially detected.

What begins as a technical investigation becomes a race to preserve evidence from an organization capable of rewriting records, erasing infrastructure, and burying the truth inside layers of government secrecy.

The server farm is not the source. It is only the first node.

The Trilogy

The Compute-Energy Nexus

Three hard-science-fiction techno-thrillers. Each book resolves a principal investigation while advancing a larger mythology.

NRO analyst Jason Cavanaugh and DTRA physicist Dr. Joanne Sanderson investigate failures in which information systems, energy behavior, and physical reality stop agreeing. Their cases gradually reveal a hidden Antarctic infrastructure that may be maintaining the physical state of human civilization.

How It Was Built

Human-directed, AI-assisted fiction

The Sub-Zero Vector was created through collaboration between the author and artificial intelligence tools. AI supported portions of planning, drafting, revision, and editorial work.

Kalamazoo directed the story’s conception and development, selected and revised the material, resolved narrative and factual decisions, and approved the final manuscript. Responsibility for the published work rests with the author.
